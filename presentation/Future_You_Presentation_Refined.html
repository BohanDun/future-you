The customer problem

Banking explains the past. Customers also need help testing the next decision.

Most banking tools answer:
“What happened to my money?”

Customers also need to know:
“What happens to my goals if I make this decision?”

What we built

Ask a financial “what-if” question and receive a quantified answer based on your real plan.

1. Understand the decision Bedrock extracts the amount, timing and intended goal from the question.
→
2. Calculate the impact A deterministic Python engine projects cash flow, goal timing and financial risk.
→
3. Explain the trade-off The customer sees what changes, what stays unchanged and why.

Every displayed financial figure is calculated by the rules engine. The language model never invents financial amounts.

Responsible agent design

Safe actions by design.

Advice

Runs a scenario without changing the saved plan.

The customer can explore a decision and its trade-offs without writing any data.

Manage

Previews the exact proposed change and requires explicit confirmation.

Only a validated, confirmed proposal can update the customer’s saved plan.

Never

Transfers money, makes payments or silently changes customer data.

Financial execution remains outside the agent’s permitted actions.
